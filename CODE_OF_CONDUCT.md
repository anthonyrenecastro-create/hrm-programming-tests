# Code of Conduct

This project follows a respectful, inclusive collaboration model.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus critiques on ideas and implementations, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Reporting

Open a private issue or contact maintainers directly to report concerns.
